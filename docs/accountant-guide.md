# Accountant Guide (placeholder)

**Status**: Stage 1 placeholder. Full content authored in Stage 15 (T249).

This file will become the day-to-day workflow guide for accountants and bookkeepers. Scope (per [tasks.md](../specs/008-egypt-tax-accounting/tasks.md) T249):

- US1 — Issue sales invoices, generate PDFs, view ETA submission status, scan QR seal.
- US2 — Record purchase invoices and expenses, attach supporting documents, classify deductible vs non-deductible.
- US3 — Submit / approve / reject documents through the workflow.
- US4 — View auto-generated journals + create manual adjusting entries.
- US5 — Configure VAT categories, deductible categories, chart of accounts, fiscal year.
- US6 — Capitalize fixed assets, view depreciation schedule (Phase 4).
- US7 — Record WHT on supplier payments, accept customer-issued WHT certificates, generate Form 41 (نموذج 41).
- US8 — External-firm portal usage: switch between client companies, lock for review.
- US9 — Generate the Tax-Inspection Bundle for a fiscal period.
- Active ETA Compliance Dashboard, WHT Lifecycle Dashboard, Closing Cockpit walk-throughs.
- Tax Risk Score per document — interpreting findings and the "How to fix" hints.
- Issuing credit notes against posted invoices.
- Recording supplier payments + customer receipts (minimal Phase 3 payments per FR-051..FR-053).

Until Stage 15, see [specs/008-egypt-tax-accounting/spec.md](../specs/008-egypt-tax-accounting/spec.md) §"User Scenarios & Testing" for the canonical user-story acceptance scenarios.
